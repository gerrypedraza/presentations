Related Applications

Beamer to LTX Converter

Turns existing LaTeX Beamer slide decks into tagged, accessible PDF presentations by converting them to the ltx-talk class, so you can keep your course content instead of rebuilding it.

Web app, currently in beta. Sign in with a Google or approved .edu account; avoid uploading confidential material.

Templates & Examples

LaTeX Accessible Template

A LaTeX preamble that produces a tagged PDF 2.0 file meeting PDF/UA-2, with equations stored as MathML.

Start your document from it and compile with LuaLaTeX (on Overleaf, choose the Rolling TeX Live version).

Accessible Template Example In Use

A full document built on the template, with headings, lists, tables, figures with alt text, equations, and decorative content. Opens the finished PDF (PDF 2.0, PDF/UA-2).

Download the source (.zip): the LaTeX file and its images folder, ready to upload to Overleaf.
